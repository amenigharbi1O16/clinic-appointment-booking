| 2026-11-23 | Review of my user stories | Sent my user stories and asked for feedback | Feedback on my existing user stories, with suggested additions and clarifications related to reception booking, the cancellation code, appointment overlap, and availability consistency | I reviewed the suggestions and incorporated the ones that matched my project, while keeping my own roles, scope, and decisions | How to make my user stories clearer and how to distinguish user goals from technical/system rules such as concurrency and consistency |
